## Being a PhD

Guidance on succeeding as a PhD student — including Dredze & Wallach on becoming successful, Azuma's reflective essay, and curated collections of advice from [pliang279](https://github.com/pliang279/awesome-phd-advice) and [jbhuang0604](https://github.com/jbhuang0604/awesome-tips).

## Research statement

A sample research statement is available at [jfrankle.com](https://www.jfrankle.com/).

## Fellowship

Funding opportunities include NSF's [GRFP](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/) for graduates and [CSGrad4US](https://beta.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/cs-grad4us).

## Reading papers

Strategies encompass JHU's technical paper reading guide, poster review techniques via jkk.name, ACL mentorship programs, and Zotero reference management setup. The JHU CLSP NLP Reading Group provides community engagement.

## Seminars

The ISI-NLG Seminars offer ongoing research presentations.

## Writing

Guidance covers foundational concepts like "shitty first drafts," structural approaches from Harvard, style recommendations from Boyd-Graber, and NSF proposal frameworks.

## Talks

Resources address colloquium presentation skills and job talks through MIT EECS faculty tips and MIT Comm Lab resources, including "Dr. Karen's Rules of the Job Talk."

## Research

Discussion of using closed AI models as baselines in academic work.

## People's experience

Multiple researchers share job-market experiences, including guides from Wilson, Goldner, and Herbert on tenure-track positions.

## Time management

Graham's essay on productive procrastination and RescueTime tracking software support workflow optimization.

## Compute

Resources identify GPU availability sources through community discussions.
